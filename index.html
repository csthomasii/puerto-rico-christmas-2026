1Fajardo · East Coast

Fajardo · 71 minutes from SJU

Las Casitas Village at El Conquistador

  • King bedroom + living room with large twin sofa bed
  • Sleeps 4
  • Butler service
  • Water park and Palomino Island ferry
At the property
Rooms

Las Casitas is the villa side of El Conquistador: 122 villas with one to three bedrooms, a living room with a sofa bed, and a full kitchen with stove, oven, dishwasher and refrigerator. The one-bedroom ocean-view villa priced here is 840 sq ft. Expedia's listing says cribs are not available, and this property has far fewer recent reviews than the other five. lascasitasvillage.com

Food

Vinos & Tapas, a Spanish tapas room, is inside the village. The main resort adds Cutz (steak and seafood), Bono (Spanish), Sicilia (Italian), Okinawa (teppanyaki), Ballyhoo (casual seafood at the marina), La Ezkina (Mexican and Puerto Rican street food) and El Cafecito for coffee and pastries. Breakfast is not included: Ventanas posts a continental at $14 and hot plates from $16, and Expedia lists a child's breakfast at about $8. Reviewers describe resort dining as expensive. Resort dining page

Service

The village has its own concierge and butler service, separate from the main hotel's front desk. In an April 2026 Reddit thread several guests reported unanswered calls and emails before arrival, with better service once on site. TripAdvisor reviewers (1,704, rated 4.3 of 5) praise the ferry crew and villa staff and complain about waiting for golf carts at busy times. Reddit · TripAdvisor

Pools

Coquí Water Park at the main resort has slides, a lazy river and a children's area, open 9 am to 5:30 pm, and is included for resort guests. The village also has its own quieter infinity pool away from the water park. Coquí Water Park

Beach

There is no beach at the resort itself. The sand is on Palomino Island, reached by the resort boat, which leaves every hour from 9 am until 3 pm and returns until 5 pm; the island has calm water, loungers and a beach bar. Some reviewers call the boat queue the weak point of the stay. Palomino Island · TripAdvisor

Surroundings

Nothing is walkable from the resort. Expedia's map puts Seven Seas beach at a 6-minute drive, Luquillo Beach at 28 minutes and the airport at 71 minutes, and bioluminescent-bay kayak tours start within a mile in Las Croabas. Expedia

2Río Grande · Coco Beach

Río Grande · 30 minutes from SJU

Hyatt Regency Grand Reserve

  • 689 sq ft
  • King bedroom + living area with sleeper sofa
  • Sleeps 4
  • Largest lagoon pool on the island
At the property
Rooms

Rooms sit in low two-storey buildings spread across the grounds rather than a tower. Standard rooms are 520 sq ft with a king or two doubles; the Deluxe Villa is 685 sq ft and the Premier Villa 1,075 sq ft with a separate living room and a queen sofa bed. TripAdvisor reviewers (4,065) praise the quiet; recurring complaints are thin connecting doors and a damp smell in some ground-floor rooms. Expedia room list · TripAdvisor

Food

Water's Edge serves the breakfast buffet and casual meals, Prime 787 is the steakhouse, Nori Asian Grill does sushi and teppanyaki, and The Market sells coffee and grab-and-go food. Breakfast is not included; Expedia lists the buffet at about $33 an adult and $18 a child. Reviewers rate Prime 787 and Nori well and call the buffet ordinary for the price. Matador Network · TripAdvisor

Service

Praise centres on friendly staff and the golf-cart shuttle around the grounds. Complaints: check-in waits, housekeeping that ignores do-not-disturb signs, and the resort fee, 18 percent of the room rate, which a 2025 reviewer called steep on cash stays. TripAdvisor · Single Flyer

Pools

Four pools, including what is billed as the largest lagoon-style pool on the island, with a swim-up bar. The grounds are large enough that chairs are rarely a problem. Matador Network

Beach

The beach is sand and palms, but the water is not good for swimming: reviewers report seagrass, a rocky bottom and sizeable waves even on calm days. Kayaks are available to borrow. Guests who want to swim drive to Luquillo Beach, about 15 minutes east. Single Flyer · Oyster

Surroundings

The resort is gated and nothing is walkable; plan on a car. El Yunque and the Luquillo food kiosks are short drives east. Expedia puts the airport at 42 minutes; the 30 minutes in the header is the hotel's own figure. Expedia

3San Juan · Puerta de Tierra

San Juan · 15 minutes from SJU

Caribe Hilton

  • 850 sq ft
  • Separate living room
  • Sleeps 4
  • Walk to Old San Juan
At the property
Rooms

652 rooms in three wings; standard rooms run 338 to 377 sq ft with a king or two doubles, and the Junior Suite (463 sq ft) adds a sofa bed and balcony. The hotel reopened after a $150 million post-hurricane renovation. TripAdvisor reviewers (1,509) still describe some Wave and Garden Wing rooms as dated, and Beach Wing rooms pick up music from the pool deck at weekends. Caribe Hilton · TripAdvisor

Food

Caribar (cocktails and tapas, where the piña colada was first mixed), Nectar del Caribe (breakfast), Morton's The Steakhouse, Rustica (Italian), Lola's (Puerto Rican), the Bagua swim-up grill, a Starbucks and an ice-cream counter. Breakfast is not included; Expedia lists it at about $35 an adult and $18 a child, and reviewers call it overpriced and crowded between 8:30 and 10 am. Dining page · TripAdvisor

Service

Reviewers praise the bell staff and pool attendants. The resort fee is $54.50 a night on Expedia and is waived on Hilton Honors points stays; complaints centre on late-afternoon check-in lines and on the fee itself. NerdWallet · TripAdvisor

Pools

Oceanfront pools on two levels, open 7 am to 10 pm: a 5-foot-deep infinity pool, the Bagua pool with a swim-up bar, and the Tropical Pool beside the hammock garden, plus hot tubs. Pool and beach page

Beach

The hotel has its own beach on the Isleta de San Juan, reached by bridge from Condado; the NerdWallet reviewer rated it the best part of the stay and quieter than Condado Beach. Balneario El Escambrón, a public beach with lifeguards and snorkelling, is a short walk. NerdWallet · Snorkel and Hike

Surroundings

Old San Juan is just across the bridge: about a 40-minute walk or a 5 to 8 minute ride. Condado's restaurants are a similar short ride the other way. Expedia puts the airport at 12 minutes. NerdWallet · Expedia

4San Juan · Condado

Condado · 12 minutes from SJU

Condado Vanderbilt Hotel

  • King bedroom + separate living room with sofa bed
  • Sleeps 4
  • Children under 12 stay free
  • Beachfront, 1919 landmark
At the property
Rooms

Two room types: Heritage rooms in the 1919 building (323 sq ft, city view) and Tower rooms with ocean views (450 to 480 sq ft). One-bedroom Commodore suites are 818 sq ft with a kitchenette and sofa bed; two-bedroom suites are about 1,275 sq ft. TripAdvisor reviewers (3,265) say the Heritage rooms feel small once the sofa bed is out, and lower city-side floors hear Ashford Avenue. condadovanderbilt.com · TripAdvisor

Food

1919 (tasting menus), STK (steakhouse and lounge, loud in the evening), Ola Oceanfront Bistro (all day, with the breakfast buffet), Tacos & Tequila, the VC Lounge and Veritas wine bar. Breakfast is not included: Expedia lists it at $35 to $50 an adult and $25 to $35 a child. The resort fee is 20 percent of the room rate. Dining page · Expedia

Service

Reviewers praise the pool and beach attendants and the concierge. Complaints: rooms not ready until after 5 pm on busy days, and slow valet retrieval in the evening. TripAdvisor

Pools

A main oceanfront infinity pool, an adults-only infinity pool on the tower deck and smaller sundeck pools; the hotel's own documents count three, some listings say four. One TripAdvisor review calls the pool area small. TripAdvisor

Beach

The sand in front is rocky with strong surf and is not for swimming; Oyster calls it tiny and unsuitable, and TripAdvisor answers to "can you swim at this beach" say the same. The hotel sets out loungers and umbrellas on a sand deck. Families walk 5 to 10 minutes to Playita del Condado, a sheltered cove. Oyster · TripAdvisor Q&A

Surroundings

Ashford Avenue's restaurants, shops and pharmacies start at the door. Old San Juan is about 1.5 miles: a 30 to 40 minute walk or a 10 minute ride. Expedia puts the airport at 12 minutes. Oyster · Expedia

5Río Grande · Rio Mar

Río Grande · 35 minutes from SJU

Wyndham Grand Rio Mar

  • 1,274 sq ft
  • King bedroom, bunk-bed alcove for the kids, living room with sofa bed
  • Built for families
  • El Yunque next door, two golf courses
At the property
Rooms

A $60 million renovation finished in late 2025, so rooms, pools and restaurants are new. Standard rooms are about 475 sq ft with a balcony and a king or two queens; the Discovery family suites (1,274 sq ft) add a bunk-bed alcove and a living-room sofa bed. Recent TripAdvisor reviews (4,444 in total) mention noisy air-conditioning fans and some rooms facing the car park. Hotel News Resource · TripAdvisor

Food

Roots Coastal Kitchen (breakfast 6:30 to 11, plus lunch and dinner), Palio (Italian steakhouse, no children under 6), Amaya (Spanish tapas), Iguanas (Puerto Rican, at the golf clubhouse), Caicu (rum bar) and Seabreeze (casual, outdoors). Breakfast is not included; Expedia lists the buffet at $32 to $42 an adult and $16 to $26 a child. The resort fee is 24 percent of the room rate. Dining page · Expedia

Service

Praise: turn-down service, the activity staff and the concierge. Complaints: daily housekeeping has to be requested rather than assumed, and self-parking (about $25 a day) is charged on top of the resort fee. TripAdvisor · She Buys Travel

Pools

Five pools after the renovation: Turtle Cove Adventure Pool (slides), Coquí Splash Pool for small children, the Beach Club pool by the sand, a Margaritaville LandShark pool, and Aura, an adults-only pool open 10 am to 6 pm. Pools page

Beach

A long golden-sand beach on the open Atlantic with loungers, umbrellas and beach bars. Waves and undertow are common in winter and flags fly often, so it suits wading more than swimming for young children; guests who want calm water drive to Luquillo Beach, the next town east. TripAdvisor · Resort FAQ

Surroundings

A gated resort with nothing walkable; reviewers say a car is a must even with the parking charge. The hotel calls itself the closest resort to El Yunque, and Luquillo's food kiosks are the next exit. The hotel says about 30 minutes from SJU; Expedia says 47. Resort FAQ · Expedia

6San Juan · Condado

Condado · 12 minutes from SJU

La Concha Resort, Autograph Collection

  • King bedroom + living room with sofa bed
  • Kitchenette
  • Ocean-view balcony
  • Marriott Bonvoy
At the property
Rooms

Two towers. Ocean Tower rooms are 396 sq ft; the Suite Tower's 238 rooms were redone in the $80.2 million renovation, and its one-bedroom Deluxe Suites (570 to 580 sq ft) have a living room with a sofa bed, some with a kitchenette. The hotel doubles as a nightlife venue: TripAdvisor reviewers (9,118) report music from the lobby bar and pool deck reaching lower Ocean Tower floors late at night, so ask for a high floor in the Suite Tower. The Points Guy · Hotel Online · TripAdvisor

Food

Levant by chef Michael White (Mediterranean, inside the shell-shaped Perla building), AQA Oceanfront (breakfast and brunch buffet), Serafina (Italian), Solera (poolside tapas and tacos), Tekka Bar (hand-roll sushi), Aroma Cafe (coffee, pastries, breakfast sandwiches) and the lobby bar. Breakfast is not included; Expedia lists it at $44 an adult and $20 a child, or $85 a night for two as a rate add-on. Dining page · Expedia

Service

Expedia reviewers (9.2 of 10) praise the pool and beach servers and the valet. Complaints: slow front desk at check-in and check-out, a larger deposit hold than expected, and staff attention that leans toward nightlife guests. Expedia · TripAdvisor

Pools

Three pools normally: the main multi-level infinity pool (music, cocktail service, busy in the afternoon), Marena, an adults-only pool, and the Sereno beach pool, which is closed for a rebuild until 20 December 2026, three days before this stay. There is no children's splash area. Amenities page

Beach

Direct access to Condado Beach, with chairs, umbrellas and towels from the hotel. The Points Guy says the currents can be strong and the water suits confident swimmers; TripAdvisor reviewers report red flags and shore break. Playita del Condado, a short walk, and Escambrón Beach, a short drive, have calmer water. The Points Guy · TripAdvisor

Surroundings

On Ashford Avenue in the middle of Condado: restaurants, shops and a supermarket are on foot. Old San Juan is about 3 miles, a 10 minute ride, and Expedia puts the airport at 11 minutes. Expedia · TripAdvisor

Fine Print

What the numbers include

Taxes and fees

Puerto Rico charges a 9% room tax at hotels without a casino and 11% at those with one. Expedia totals on this page already include tax and the resort fee. Booking.com totals include the resort fee only, so the estimated tax is shown alongside.

Refundable or not

Non-refundable rates run 10 to 20 percent below flexible ones. Flexible rates at most of these properties can be cancelled into December, so booking flexible now and re-checking in November keeps every option open.

Programs and cards

Each card lists the hotel's loyalty program, whether it sits in Amex Fine Hotels + Resorts (Platinum) or The Hotel Collection (Platinum and Gold), and whether it is in Chase Sapphire Reserve's The Edit. Only Condado Vanderbilt is in all three worlds; Hyatt and Wyndham carry the Amex Hotel Collection credit; Las Casitas, Caribe Hilton, and La Concha are in none of the card collections. Checked 3 September 2026.

Before you pay

Ask each property whether a Christmas Eve or Christmas Day dinner is mandatory and what it costs. Confirm that two adults and two children are accepted in the room you pick, and note that holiday bookings usually become non-refundable 30 to 60 days out.
